# IGNITION Platform - Executive Summary

## The Problem We Solve

Token creators on Solana face two challenges:
1. **High launch costs** - Most launchers charge 1-2+ SOL
2. **No ongoing incentives** - Once launched, holders have no reason to stay

IGNITION solves both with a self-sustaining "flywheel" ecosystem.

---

## How It Works

### Ultra-Low-Cost Token Launching
- **Deploy for just 0.02 SOL** on Pump.fun
- All tokens get unique **ASDF vanity addresses** (mint ends with `ASDF`)
- Full metadata, images, and social links supported

### The Flywheel: Automated Rewards

Every token in the IGNITION ecosystem generates rewards for its holders:

```
Trading Fees → Airdrop Pool → SOL Distributed to Holders
     ↑                              ↓
     └────── More Trading ←─────────┘
```

**Fee Collection:** Every 2.5 minutes, trading fees are automatically collected
**Airdrop Distribution:** Every 15 minutes, SOL is airdropped to all eligible holders

---

## Point System (Supply-Based)

Your airdrop share is based on your **% of total token supply**:

```
Points = (Your Balance / 1 Billion) × Token's Weighted Points
```

- **Volume Weighting:** Higher-volume tokens earn 0.5x to 2.0x more points
- **Eligibility:** Tokens need >$100 24hr volume
- **Fair:** Based on actual ownership, not just tracked holders

### Example
If you hold 10 million tokens (1% of 1B supply) of a high-volume token:
- Base points: 1,000 × 1% = 10 points
- With 2.0x volume multiplier: 20 points
- With ASDF 2x multiplier: 40 points

---

## Robinhood Tokens: Fee-Sharing Partnerships

External Pump.fun creators can join the ecosystem:

1. Creator enables fee sharing on Pump.fun (adds IGNITION as recipient)
2. Token is registered in IGNITION
3. **All holders of that token now earn airdrop points**

Points are scaled by the creator's fee share percentage:
- 100% share = Full points
- 50% share = Half points

This creates a network effect where more tokens = more fees = bigger airdrops.

### ASDF Multiplier
- **Top 100 ASDF token holders get 2x points**
- Rewards early supporters and ecosystem participation
