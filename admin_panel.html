Points Breakdown

How Points Are Calculated

Base Points: 1000 points per eligible token held

Volume Weight: 0.5x - 2.0x multiplier based on 24hr token volume

Formula: Points = 1000 * volumeWeight (per token)

Volume Weight Scale

Low Volume: 0.5x multiplier (500 points)

Medium Volume: 1.0x multiplier (1000 points)

High Volume: 2.0x multiplier (2000 points)

Volume weight is calculated using logarithmic scale between min and max volume across all tokens.
