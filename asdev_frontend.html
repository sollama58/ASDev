SOL Airdrop System

Volume-Weighted Airdrops

Airdrops are distributed in SOL proportionally to your holdings. Higher-volume tokens reward their holders more since they contribute more fees to the pool.

Timing

Fees collected every 1 minute (>0.05 SOL). Airdrops distributed every 15 minutes (>1.0 SOL pool).

Points are calculated based on your proportional holdings in eligible tokens:

  • Eligibility: Top 250 holders of tokens with >$100 24hr volume earn points.
  • Volume Weight: Each token's base points are scaled by its 24hr volume relative to other eligible tokens (0.5x to 2.0x multiplier).
  • Base Points: Each eligible token distributes 500-2000 points (based on volume) proportionally among top 250 holders.
  • Robinhood Bonus: +Points for holding partner tokens (scaled by volume + fee share %).
  • ASDF Multiplier: 2x Total Points if you're a Top 100 $ASDF holder.

Volume Weight Formula

Weight = 0.5 + 1.5 × (log(tokenVol) - log(minVol)) / (log(maxVol) - log(minVol))

Lowest volume token = 0.5x, Highest volume token = 2.0x (logarithmic scale)

Example

Token A ($50k volume, max) = 2.0x weight = 2000 base points. If you hold 10% of top 250 supply = 200 points. With ASDF multiplier = 400 points.

Token B ($1k volume, min) = 0.5x weight = 500 base points. Same 10% holding = 50 points.

King of the Pill (KOTH)

10% of every airdrop goes to holders of the highest market cap token, proportional to their holdings.

Fee Claim & Rewards Mechanics

1. Revenue Generation

The platform generates revenue through two primary streams:

  • Deployment Fee: 0.02 SOL per token launch.
  • Trading Rewards: 1% of the bonding curve volume (Creator Fee).

2. The Flywheel (Fee Collection)

Every 1 minute, if pending fees > 0.05 SOL:

  • 95.0%: Added to SOL Airdrop Pool (for direct distribution).
  • 4.5%: Sent to $ASDF Burns Wallet.
  • 0.5%: Sent to Upkeep and Reserves Wallet.

3. Volume-Weighted Points System

Points are distributed proportionally based on your holdings. Higher-volume tokens reward more points since they contribute more fees.

  • Eligibility: Top 250 holders of tokens with >$100 24hr volume.
  • Volume Weight: Each token's points scaled by volume (0.5x to 2.0x) relative to other tokens.
  • Base Points: Each eligible token distributes 500-2000 points (based on volume) proportionally.
  • Robinhood Bonus: +Points for holding partner tokens (scaled by volume + fee share %).
  • Multiplier: 2x Total Points if you are a Top 100 $ASDF holder.

King of the Pill

10% of the entire airdrop pool is distributed to all holders of the highest market cap token, proportional to their holdings.

4. Automated Airdrop Engine

Runs every 15 minutes automatically.

  • Trigger: When SOL Airdrop Pool > 1.0 SOL (after 0.5 SOL reserve).
  • Action: Distributes 99% of the SOL pool directly to all qualified users.
  • Distribution: Proportional based on your share of the Total Global Points (minus 10% KOTH allocation).

How Robinhood Works

PumpFun Creator Rewards Sharing

PumpFun allows token creators to share their 1% creator fees with other wallets. When another creator adds our wallet as a fee shareholder, we automatically detect it and include their token in our ecosystem.

Automatic Detection

  • Our bot monitors the Solana blockchain for fee sharing configs
  • When a token shares fees with us, it's automatically added as a "Robinhood Token"
  • No manual registration required - fully automated

How You Benefit

  • Proportional Points based on your holdings (Top 100 holders)
  • Volume Weight: Higher volume tokens = more points (0.5x to 2.0x)
  • Fee Share Scaling: Points multiplied by fee share % (50% share = 50% points)
  • These points stack with your regular leaderboard holdings
  • More Robinhood partnerships = more airdrop opportunities

For Token Creators

  • Share fees with our wallet to join the Robinhood network
  • Your holders gain airdrop eligibility automatically
  • Higher volume = more points for your holders (rewards active trading)
  • Creates additional incentives for holders to stay in your token

Volume-Weighted Formula

Robinhood Points = (Your Balance / Top 100 Balance) × Volume Weight × Fee Share % × 1000

Volume Weight ranges from 0.5x (lowest vol) to 2.0x (highest vol) using logarithmic scale

Total Points Formula

(Platform Holdings × Vol Weight + Robinhood Holdings × Vol Weight × Fee%) × ASDF Multiplier
