SOL Airdrop System

Volume-Weighted Airdrops

Airdrops are distributed in SOL proportionally to your holdings. Higher-volume tokens reward their holders more since they contribute more fees to the pool.

Timing

Fees collected every 2.5 minutes (>0.05 SOL). Airdrops distributed every 15 minutes (>1.0 SOL pool).

Points are calculated based on your % of total token supply in eligible tokens:

  • Eligibility: Top 250 holders of tokens with >$100 24hr volume earn points.
  • Volume Weight: Each token's base points are scaled by its 24hr volume relative to other eligible tokens (0.5x to 2.0x multiplier).
  • Supply-Based Points: Points = (Your Balance / 1B Total Supply) × Token's Weighted Points. All pump.fun tokens have 1 billion total supply.
  • Robinhood Bonus: +Points for holding partner tokens (scaled by volume + fee share %).
  • ASDF Multiplier: 2x Total Points if you're a Top 100 $ASDF holder.

Point Formula (v25.36)

Points = (Your Balance / 1,000,000,000) × Volume Weight × 1000

Based on % of total supply, not % of tracked holders. Fair and transparent!

Example

Token A ($50k volume, max) = 2.0x weight = 2000 base points. If you hold 1% of total supply (10M tokens) = 20 points. With ASDF multiplier = 40 points.

Token B ($1k volume, min) = 0.5x weight = 500 base points. Same 1% holding = 5 points.

Fee Claim & Rewards Mechanics

1. Revenue Generation

The platform generates revenue through two primary streams:

  • Deployment Fee: 0.02 SOL per token launch.
  • Trading Rewards: 1% of the bonding curve volume (Creator Fee).

2. The Flywheel (Fee Collection)

Every 2.5 minutes, if pending fees > 0.05 SOL:

  • 95.0%: Added to SOL Airdrop Pool (for direct distribution).
  • 4.5%: Sent to $ASDF Burns Wallet.
  • 0.5%: Sent to Upkeep and Reserves Wallet.

3. Supply-Based Points System

Points are based on your % of total token supply. Higher-volume tokens reward more points since they contribute more fees.

  • Eligibility: Top 250 holders of tokens with >$100 24hr volume.
  • Volume Weight: Each token's points scaled by volume (0.5x to 2.0x) relative to other tokens.
  • Supply-Based: Points = (Your Balance / 1B Supply) × Weighted Points. All pump.fun tokens have 1B supply.
  • Robinhood Bonus: +Points for holding partner tokens (scaled by volume + fee share %).
  • Multiplier: 2x Total Points if you are a Top 100 $ASDF holder.

King of the Pill AI POWERED

10% of the entire airdrop pool is distributed to holders of the AI-selected champion token.

Claude AI evaluates the top 10 leaderboard tokens every 30 minutes using volume, market cap, holder growth, and token age.

4. Automated Airdrop Engine

Runs every 15 minutes automatically.

  • Trigger: When SOL Airdrop Pool > 1.0 SOL (after 0.5 SOL reserve).
  • Action: Distributes 99% of the SOL pool directly to all qualified users.
  • Distribution: Proportional based on your share of the Total Global Points (minus 10% KOTH allocation).

Robinhood Partnership Program

What is Robinhood?

Robinhood is our partnership program for external token creators. When creators share their PumpFun trading fees with IGNITION and register their token, it joins our ecosystem and their holders become eligible for SOL airdrops.

For Token Creators - How to Join

  1. Set up fee sharing on PumpFun: Go to your Creator Dashboard and add IGNITION's wallet as a fee recipient
  2. Register your token: Use the "Register Token" button on our platform to submit your token's mint address
  3. Verification: Our system verifies that fee sharing is properly configured
  4. Activation: Once verified, your token is added and holders start earning points

Note: Manual registration is required - tokens are not automatically detected.

Benefits for Holders

  • Top 250 Holders earn points based on % of total supply (1B tokens)
  • Volume Multiplier: Higher volume = more points (0.5x to 2.0x scale)
  • Fee Share %: Points scaled by creator's fee share (e.g., 50% share = 50% points)
  • Points stack with platform token holdings for maximum earnings
  • SOL airdrops distributed based on total point share

Why Manual Registration?

  • Ensures only legitimate projects join the network
  • Prevents spam tokens from diluting rewards
  • Allows creators to choose when to activate rewards
  • Verifies fee sharing is correctly configured before activation

Volume Weight: 0.5x (min volume) to 2.0x (max volume) - logarithmic scale

All pump.fun tokens have a fixed 1 billion total supply

Total Points (Combined)

(Platform Points + Robinhood Points) × ASDF Multiplier

ASDF Multiplier: 2x boost for Top 50 ASDF holders
