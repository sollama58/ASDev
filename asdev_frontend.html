SOL AIRDROP SYSTEM (v17.0)

💰 PROPORTIONAL AIRDROPS

Airdrops are distributed in **SOL** proportionally to your holdings. The more you hold, the more you earn!

⏱️ TIMING

Fees collected every 1 minute (>0.05 SOL) | Airdrops distributed every 15 minutes (>1.0 SOL pool)

Points are calculated based on your **proportional holdings** in eligible tokens:

  • Eligibility: Top 250 holders of tokens with >$100 24hr volume earn points.
  • Base Points: Each eligible token distributes 1000 points proportionally based on your balance vs total holdings.
  • Creator Bonus: 2x Points on tokens you created (if you're also a top holder).
  • Robinhood Bonus: +Points for holding partner tokens (scaled by fee share %).
  • ASDF Multiplier: 2x Total Points if you're a Top 100 $ASDF holder.

📊 EXAMPLE

If you hold 10% of a token's top 250 supply, you get 100 points from that token. If you created it, that's 200 points!

💊 KING OF THE PILL (KOTH)

10% of every airdrop goes to holders of the highest MCAP token, proportional to their holdings!

FEE & BUYBACK MECHANICS

1. REVENUE GENERATION

The platform generates revenue through two primary streams:

  • Deployment Fee: 0.02 SOL per token launch.
  • Trading Rewards: 1% of the bonding curve volume (Creator Fee).

2. THE FLYWHEEL (FEE COLLECTION)

Every 1 minute, if pending fees > 0.05 SOL:

  • 95.0%: Added to **SOL Airdrop Pool** (for direct distribution).
  • 4.5%: Sent to $ASDF Burns Wallet.
  • 0.5%: Sent to Upkeep and Reserves Wallet.

3. PROPORTIONAL POINTS SYSTEM (v18.0)

Points are distributed proportionally based on your holdings. No signup required.

  • Eligibility: Top 250 holders of tokens with >$100 24hr volume.
  • Base Points: Each eligible token distributes 1000 points proportionally to holdings.
  • Creator Bonus: 2x Points on tokens you created.
  • Multiplier: 2x Total Points if you are a Top 100 Holder of **$ASDF**.

💊 KING OF THE PILL

10% of the entire Airdrop pot is distributed to all holders of the highest Market Cap token, proportional to their holdings!

4. AUTOMATED AIRDROP ENGINE

Runs every 15 minutes automatically.

  • Trigger: When SOL Airdrop Pool > 1.0 SOL (after 0.5 SOL reserve).
  • Action: Distributes **99%** of the SOL pool directly to all qualified users.
  • Distribution: Proportional based on your share of the Total Global Points (minus 10% KOTH allocation).

PumpFun now allows token creators to share their 1% creator fees with other wallets. When another creator adds our wallet as a fee shareholder, we automatically detect it and include their token in our ecosystem.

AUTOMATIC DETECTION

  • When a token shares fees with us, it's automatically added as a "Robinhood Token"
  • No manual registration required - fully automated
