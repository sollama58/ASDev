💩 About ShitPad
Token launchpads are shit.

Every one of them takes a cut of your trades and gives you a leaderboard. ShitPad takes a cut too — we're not pretending otherwise — but 75% of it goes back on-chain, and the rest buys and burns.

Mechanically: when a Pump.fun creator sets the ShitPad wallet as a fee recipient, a portion of every trade flows in. Every 15 minutes it's pushed out to token holders automatically. No claims, no points, no vesting, no waiting.

How the Pool Works
💸
Fee collection: Every trade on a ShitPad token generates a fee. ShitPad mints each token with itself as a fee recipient, so that fee arrives at the platform wallet on-chain through Pump.fun's fee-sharing mechanism, with nothing for the creator to configure.
⚖️
50 / 25 / 25 fee split: Each time fees are collected from a token they are split three ways — 50% into that token's Individual Pool (paid out exclusively to holders of that token), 25% into the Central Pool (shared across every ShitPad token's holder base), and 25% to buyback & burn. Your token's own volume directly rewards your holders, while activity anywhere on the platform tops up everyone.
🔥
Buyback & burn: The platform's 25% accrues on-chain and is swept to the buyback/burn wallet in batches. It's the only cut ShitPad keeps, and it's the one that shrinks supply rather than funding a dashboard.
🏦
Two pools, two payouts: At each airdrop cycle, a holder's total expected SOL comes from both pools — their share of the Central Pool (based on holdings across all eligible tokens) plus their share of any Individual Pools for tokens they hold. The amounts are summed and sent in a single transaction.
🎯
Airdrop distribution: Every 15 minutes, eligible pools are distributed to holders proportional to their token holdings. SOL lands directly in wallets — no action required.
✅
Eligibility: A token must have ≥ $250 in 24-hour trading volume to receive a distribution in a given cycle. Tokens below this threshold still accumulate fees in both pools but skip that cycle's drop.
For Token Creators

Launching is open to anyone. Connect a wallet, fill in the name, ticker and image, and sign the launch fee. ShitPad mints the token on Pump.fun with fee sharing already wired to the platform wallet, so there is nothing to configure afterwards. Your holders start receiving SOL airdrops in the next cycle.

Where the address pool has one ready, your contract address ends in shit. If the pool is empty the launch still goes ahead with an ordinary address rather than making you wait.

For Token Holders

Simply hold tokens launched on ShitPad. SOL distributions are sent directly to your wallet based on your proportional share of the token's supply (excluding LP and burnt tokens). You can track expected airdrop amounts by clicking any token in the list.

A minimum distribution of 0.01 SOL per wallet applies — holders below this threshold in a given cycle are skipped to avoid dust transactions.

How Your Airdrop Amount is Calculated

Your total expected airdrop each cycle is the sum of two independent calculations — one from the Central Pool and one from each Individual Token Pool you hold.

🏦
Individual Token Pool (per-token): When a token's individual pool reaches 1 SOL, 99% is distributed to its holders.

Your share = your balance ÷ total tracked supply × distributable pool

Balances held by LP and bonding curve accounts are excluded from the denominator. ASDF Top 100 and ANSEM Top 1000 holders each have their effective balance doubled (stackable to 4× if you hold both), giving greater weight in this distribution.
⚖️
Central Pool (shared): When the central pool reaches 5 SOL, 99% is distributed across all holders of all eligible tokens. The split is market-cap-weighted — tokens with higher market cap contribute more weight to your score.

For each eligible token you hold:
score contribution = (your balance ÷ 1B supply) × market cap multiplier × ASDF bonus × ANSEM bonus

The market cap multiplier ranges from 0.5× (very low relative market cap) to 1.5× (very high relative market cap). ASDF Top 100 and ANSEM Top 1000 holders each receive an additional 2× bonus (stackable to 4×). Your final share is your total score divided by the sum of all users' scores.
➕
Your total expected airdrop = sum of your share from each individual token pool you hold + your share of the central pool. These are added together and sent as a single SOL transaction. If the combined amount is below 0.01 SOL, you are skipped for that cycle.
