💩 How ShitPad works
The short version

Every launchpad takes a cut of your trades. ShitPad takes one too — but 75% of it goes back on-chain to holders, and the rest buys back and burns. Tokens are minted on Pump.fun with ShitPad as the creator-fee recipient, so fees arrive automatically and are pushed out to holders every 15 minutes. No claiming, no points, no vesting.

Where each fee goes
50%That token's own pool, paid only to its holders.
25%The central pool, shared by holders of every eligible ShitPad token.
25%Buyback & burn — swept in batches to the burn wallet. A sliver (0.5% of the fee) covers server costs.
When pools pay out
🏦
Token pools pay when they reach 1 SOL. 99% is distributed to that token's holders in proportion to how much they hold; 1% stays as a rounding buffer.
⚖️
The central pool pays when it reaches 5 SOL. Your share depends on your holdings across every eligible token, weighted by each token's market cap (0.5× to 1.5×).
⏱️
Drops run every 15 minutes. A pool under its threshold simply keeps growing until the next cycle.
✅
Eligibility: a token needs at least $250 of trading volume in the last 24 hours to take part in a cycle. Its pool still accumulates fees while it waits.
🔤
The ASDF bonus: wallets ranked in the top 250 ASDF holders count at 2× their balance in every pool. It is the only bonus. The list refreshes every 30 minutes.
🧮
Minimum payout: a wallet's share must reach 0.01 SOL in a cycle to be sent. Smaller shares are not given to anyone else — they stay in the pool and are paid once they've grown.
🚫
Who is excluded: liquidity pools, bonding curves, program-owned accounts and the ShitPad wallet itself are never counted as holders.
The maths, if you want it
🏦
Token pool share = your effective balance ÷ all tracked effective balances × 99% of the pool. “Effective” means your balance times the ASDF bonus if you have it. Tracked balances are the top 250 wallets of that token, minus the exclusions above.
⚖️
Central pool score, for each eligible token you hold = (your balance ÷ 1B supply) × market-cap multiplier × ASDF bonus. Your share of the central pool is your total score over everyone's.
➕
Your payout = every token pool share + your central pool share, sent as one transfer.
